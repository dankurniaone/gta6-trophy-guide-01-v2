Roadmap to the Platinum

Play in this order. Story first — it unlocks most of the map and both protagonists. One warning up front: there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Only one trophy can actually be missed — see Stage 1.

    PowerPyx rates the Platinum 4/10 at about 200 hours: long, not hard. There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them.
